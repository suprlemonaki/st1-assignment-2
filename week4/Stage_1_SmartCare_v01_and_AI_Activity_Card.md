# SmartCare v0.1 - Initial Engineering Brief and AI Activity Card

*A2 Case Study Stage 1 student resource*

## SmartCare scenario

SmartCare Community Clinic currently uses spreadsheets and paper records to manage patients and appointments. The client says: 'We need software to help manage patients, practitioners and appointments.' This is not yet a complete specification.

## Initial Engineering Brief

### 1. Problem summary

SmartCare Community Clinic currently manages patients, practitioners, and appointments using spreadsheets and paper record. This causes a multitude of problems such as duplicate bookings, patient records being unlocateable, inconsistent appointments, poor visibility of doctor availability, manual cancellations, an unreliable appointment history, and difficulty producing basic reports. The clients statement, "we need software to help manage patients, doctors, and appointments" is a starting point, not a full specification. Management wants a simple first version, not a complex hospital system, so before design begins the brief needs to confirm stakeholder needs, agree which features are actually required, and identify what information is still missing.

### 2. Initial stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Possible need |
|---|---|
| Reception / admin staff | Possibly need a straightforward way to book, reschedule, and search for appointments without duplicate entries (to be confirmed). |
| GPs | Possibly need clear visibility of their own daily schedule and availability (to be confirmed). |
| Patients | Possibly need a simple way to book appointments and trust that their records are accurate and private (to be confirmed). |
| Clinic management | Possibly need basic operational reports and confidence that the system stays simple rather than becoming a full hospital system (to be confirmed). |

### 3. Initial features

| Feature | Confirmed or provisional? | Why? |
|---|---|---|
| Patient records management | Confirmed | The client statement explicitly names "patients" as something the software must help manage. |
| Doctor management | Confirmed | The client statement explicitly names "practitioners" as something the software must help manage. |
| Appointment booking / management | Confirmed | The client statement explicitly names "appointments" as something the software must help manage. |
| Appointment status tracking | Provisional | Likely needed to solve the clinic's inconsistent-status problem, but not yet directly confirmed by the client. |

### 4. Questions for the client

1. What specific tasks do you want the software to support for patients, practitioners and appointments in this first version?
2. What information should be stored for each patient and each practitioner?
3. What statuses should an appointment be able to have?
4. What kind of basic operational reports would be useful to management?
5. Who will be using the system day-to-day, and do they need different levels of access?

### 5. What we do not yet know

1. Whether reception staff, practitioners, or both will be entering and editing appointment data.
2. What specific patient and practitioner details the client actually wants recorded.
3. Whether the client wants the system to support one clinic location or several.
